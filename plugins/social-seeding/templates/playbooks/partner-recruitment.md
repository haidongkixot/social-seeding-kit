# Playbook: partner and distributor recruitment

**Status:** DRAFT. Use it for catalog categories whose goal is finding distributors or partners, not selling to buyers. Replace the examples, and keep the claims inside an APPROVED knowledge file (for example `knowledge/partners/distributors.md`). Placeholders: `{brand}`, `{rep}`, `{destination}`.

## Where this playbook applies
- **Discussion groups and pages** of business owners: threads about suppliers, importing, distribution or partnerships.
- **Distributor leads:** a seller's ad in a discussion channel, within a category of the catalog, is a candidate to approach in a comment, never in a board.

## Stage moves
| Stage | Goal | Signals to watch | Move | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 SEED | Be a credible, helpful voice | A partner or supply question | Answer the question; say in one line what {brand} does; ask about their business (city, what they distribute). No link. | Promise exclusivity, margins, contracts or permits |
| 1 ENGAGED | Learn their capacity | They replied | Ask about storage, transport, current brands or volumes (one question). | Ask for documents or personal data |
| 2 QUALIFIED | Offer the next step | They ask how to work with us, or say they have a warehouse, shop or transport | Say the details are shared in the partner group and invite them there ("Shall I send you the link?"). | Send the link before they say yes |
| 3 INVITED | Send the link | They say yes | The link for the category from `funnel.toml`, by DM if the channel has `links = "no"` or `"unknown"`. | Negotiate terms in public |
| 4 JOINED | Close the loop | The owner sees them join | Close the conversation file. | none |
| X CLOSED | Stop | `max_unanswered_turns` unanswered turns, negative words, admin note | Nothing. | Argue |

## Example patterns (replace)
**Partner question** ("Does anyone know a reliable importer of ...?"):
> Hi, I am {rep}, from {brand}. We work with {category} and are building a network of local distributors. What do you distribute today, and in which city?

**ENGAGED to QUALIFIED:**
> Thanks for the details. We share the conditions in a group for interested partners. Shall I send you the link?

## Compliance
Only wording from the approved knowledge file may be used about the partnership. If a person asks about anything the file doesn't cover (exclusivity, margins, contracts, permits, legal status), say it will be discussed directly and do not answer it in public.
